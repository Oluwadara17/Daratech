Introduction¶

This report's objective is to examine several lifestyle and demographic factors that may impact weight status while analysing a dataset related to obesity. This dataset contains a wide range of factors, including age, gender, eating habits, physical activity levels, and transportation choices (Palechor & Manotas, 2019). We want to identify identifiable patterns and trends within the dataset, which will provide substantial insights on the prevalence of obesity and its related risk factors.

Obesity is referred to as an abnormal or excessive build-up of fat resulting in a body mass index (BMI) greater than 30, is a major health problem worldwide. Alarmingly, higher-than-optimal BMI levels are expected to have caused around 5 million deaths from noncommunicable diseases (NCDs) in 2019. Furthermore, obesity rates among both adults and children have gradually risen. Between 1990 and 2022, the percentage of children and adolescents aged 5 to 19 with obesity tripled globally, from 2% to 8%, while the prevalence of obesity among adults aged 18 and older more than doubled, from 7% to 16% (World Health Organization, 2024). The dataset for this analysis was collected from the UCL Repository and made available for research purposes via this link: https://doi.org/10.24432/C5H31Z.

Structure of the Report The report is organized as follows: • The introductory section summarizes the analytical aims and the reasoning for selecting the dataset. • The next parts provide a description of the dataset, Python code for analysis, extensive code comments, and a conclusion

The dataset used in this study comes from the healthcare industry and is appropriately labeled "Estimation of Obesity Levels Based on Eating Habits and Physical Condition." It contains data that allows the assessment of obesity levels in people from Mexico, Peru, and Colombia based on their eating habits and physical condition. With 17 variables and 2111 records, the dataset contains 8 quantitative features and 11 categorical features, each of which provides unique insights into the intricate interaction of factors that influence obesity.

Significance of the Analysis¶

Obesity is a major public health issue, causing a slew of chronic diseases and imposing significant economic costs on healthcare systems throughout the world.The data and model will be used to identify important obesity risk factors and guide evidence-based interventions aimed at encouraging healthier lifestyles and lowering the prevalence of obesity-related disorders. Obesity data is critical for healthcare professionals, wellness programs, insurance companies, and public health efforts. Predictive models can help healthcare practitioners identify people who are at risk of becoming obese, allowing for early intervention and illness prevention. Wellness programs and insurance firms may customize interventions and evaluate health risks, whereas fitness facilities can design exercise regimens to specific risk profiles. Public health efforts can use findings to help shape policies and actions targeted at lowering obesity prevalence and encouraging healthier lifestyles.
