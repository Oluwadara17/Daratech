A Case Study of Parkinson's Disease Using Machine Learning for Early Diagnosis and Progression Prediction of Neurodegenerative Diseases

With Oluseye Oladotun Faloye, Adebimpe Esan, Ayodele Esan & Adedayo Sobowale — Teesside University and Federal University Oye-Ekiti

A multimodal machine learning framework for detecting Parkinson's disease at its earliest, prodromal stage, built on the Parkinson's Progression Markers Initiative dataset of over 3,500 participants. The study combines olfactory testing, genetic markers, and clinical data, comparing Random Forest and Support Vector Machine classifiers after correcting for class imbalance and feature correlation. The refined Random Forest model reached 96% accuracy using only non-invasive clinical markers, outperforming several benchmarks that rely on costlier imaging-based approaches.

Adoption and Effectiveness of E-Learning Platforms in Auchi Polytechnic

With Aliu Omowumi Hafsat

A survey-based study of e-learning adoption among 342 students at Auchi Polytechnic, examining awareness, usage patterns, and the infrastructure barriers — connectivity, electricity, cost, device access — that limit regular use. Using the Technology Acceptance Model alongside descriptive statistics and chi-square testing, the study finds that perceived usefulness and ease of use rise with academic progression, and calls for targeted policy investment in connectivity and training.

Keywords — Technology Acceptance Model, survey design, chi-square testing
